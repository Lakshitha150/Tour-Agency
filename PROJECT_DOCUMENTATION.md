## 11. Key Patterns & Rules

1. **Single Accent Rule**: Gold (`#D4AF37`) is the ONLY accent color. Used for CTAs, price tags, badges, and icons.
2. **Trust Signals**: Green emerald checkmarks (`text-emerald`) for verified/trust elements.
3. **Card Pattern**: White background, thin border, `shadow-nordic`, gentle hover lift (`hover:-translate-y-1`).
4. **Animation**: Framer Motion `whileInView` for scroll reveals, `AnimatePresence` for list transitions.
5. **Typography Hierarchy**: Playfair Display for editorial headings, Inter for everything else.
6. **Responsive Breakpoints**: Mobile-first with `sm:` (640px), `md:` (768px), `lg:` (1024px).
7. **Image Lazy Loading**: All below-fold images use `loading="lazy"`.
